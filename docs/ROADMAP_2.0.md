# YouTrader 2.0 Roadmap

## Vision

YouTrader 2.0 is a better daily improvement system, not a broader trading platform. The product loop is **Log → Review → Understand → Improve → Repeat**.

## Product Philosophy

Prioritize clarity, trust, discipline, consistency, and retention. Keep Journal, Review, Risk, and the next best action central. Do not expand optional market, social, gamification, or platform features without approved backlog work.

## Approved Epics

1. Trust, Security, and Privacy Foundation
2. Offline Reliability and Data Integrity
3. Product Focus and Information Architecture
4. Journal and Analytics Experience
5. AI Review Consolidation
6. Prop-Firm Risk Experience
7. UX System, Accessibility, and iOS Quality
8. Monetization and Subscription
9. Performance and Architecture
10. Retention, Measurement, and Scope Control

## Milestones

| Milestone | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Trust | Secure sessions, protected data/media, fail-closed AI access, verified recovery paths. |
| Daily Loop | Journal-first behavior, first-trade value, focused review, clear sync status. |
| Premium UX | Unified AI review, understandable prop risk, accessible and calm iOS experience. |
| Scale | Feature boundaries, performance budgets, privacy-safe operational metrics. |

## Implementation Phases

| Phase | Scope |
|---|---|
| A — Critical | Security, privacy, AI quota, offline integrity, entitlement and release QA. |
| B — Product Polish | Core-loop hierarchy, onboarding, Essential Five analytics, settings and paywall clarity. |
| C — UX Excellence | Today, unified AI Review, evidence/confidence, accessibility, visual simplification. |
| D — Performance & Reliability | `App.tsx` decomposition, profiling, performance budgets, operational metrics. |
| E — Future Vision | Only approved retention and long-term work after core-loop evidence. |
