# Product Vision

## Mission

Help traders improve every day through deliberate review, disciplined risk management, and trustworthy evidence.

## Target Users

Active discretionary traders, especially futures and prop-firm traders, who want a consistent system for journaling, reviewing behavior, and protecting risk.

## Core Product Promise

YouTrader is the daily system that helps traders continuously improve.

## Core Loop

**Log → Review → Understand → Improve → Repeat**

## What YouTrader Is

- A structured trade journal and review system.
- A behavioral and risk-awareness tool.
- A prop-firm risk companion.
- An educational AI assistant grounded in the user's review context.

## What YouTrader Is Not

- A brokerage, execution terminal, copy-trading network, or social feed.
- A source of autonomous buy/sell signals or financial advice.
- A general-purpose market terminal.

## Long-Term Vision

Become the trusted daily operating system for traders who want to improve process quality, discipline, and consistency over time.

## Non-Goals

Avoid feature sprawl, speculative social features, unnecessary gamification, and broad market-platform expansion. The product direction is in [ROADMAP_2.0.md](./ROADMAP_2.0.md); approved implementation work is in [BACKLOG.md](./BACKLOG.md).

## Product Principles

- Evidence before opinion.
- Clarity before density.
- Trust before growth.
- Discipline before prediction.
- One useful next action over more dashboards.
